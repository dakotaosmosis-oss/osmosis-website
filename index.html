Why we started Osmosis

Water treatment in the south metro had a pricing problem.

Ask three dealers to quote the same softener and you can hear $1,600 from one and more than $5,500 from the next, usually after a two-hour sales visit at your kitchen table. None of them post a price.

Osmosis is a Burnsville company built the other way around. We publish our prices, test your water for free, and put the results and one written plan in your hands. You decide on your own time.

We also do the work others skip. Rural Dakota and Scott county wells need more than a softener, and county testing keeps finding nitrate and PFAS in private wells. We test for both and treat what we find. When an old system of any brand acts up, we'll repair it before we try to replace it.
